FINAL STANDINGS

THE WAY OF SUMO

GOAL: Dominate the Dohyo by reducing your opponent's Will (HP) to 0 through superior technique and timing.

MECHANICS: Each turn, both Rikishi choose their action simultaneously. Kimarite consume Momentum—the spiritual energy of the match.

KIMARITE TECHNIQUES (Kimarite)

Your arsenal is selected within The Stable (部屋 - Heya). Under the Technique tab, you may customize your loadout to suit your fighting style.

  • 🥋 High & Low: Strikes are aimed at either the upper or lower body. Mastering the mix-up is key to breaking a stalwart defense.
  • ⚡ The Cost of Power: While basic strikes are free, more devastating throws and thrusts require higher Momentum.
  • 📈 Promotion: New and more powerful kamarite are unlocked as you climb the Banzuke (番付) rankings. Ascend the divisions to expand your mastery.

⚔️ CLASH RULE: If both players attack, the move with Higher Damage wins, completely canceling the weaker strike!

DEFENSE (Bogyo)

Defensive stances cost No Momentum. To survive the ring, you must predict your opponent's intent.

  • Perfect Block: Match your block height (High/Low) to the incoming kimarite to negate all damage.
  • Partial Block: A mismatched block height still provides protection, reducing incoming damage by half.

🛡️ IRON WALL: Achieving a Perfect Block activates your Iron Wall. Every consecutive Perfect Block while the wall is active instantly generates +1 Momentum!
